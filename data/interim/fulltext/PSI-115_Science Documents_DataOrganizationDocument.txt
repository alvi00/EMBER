Utilization of the Smoke Aerosol Measurement Experiment Data for Advanced Modeling and Simulation 
of Smoke Generation in Micro-Gravity 
 
To gain an initial understanding of this experiment, refer to the Final Report, located on “Pub and Docs” 
tab of this Investigation Record. The simulation data is organized in PSI in “Experiment Data” on the 
“Data” tab, in two high level categories: 
 
• 
Earth Gravity Figure Data 
• 
Microgravity Figure Data 
 
The simulation data is further categorized into specific cases: 
 
• 
Case 1 with inlet velocity: Uo = 0:08 m/s  
• 
Case 2 with inlet velocity: Uo = 0:1 m/s

In addition to the four base cases listed in Table 3 of the Final Report, and as shown above, the data is 
further categorized as the researcher examines the aerosol development sensitivity to: 
 
• 
Particle settling 
• 
Thermophoresis 
• 
The flow geometry 
 
 
For this purpose, a series of additional cases, defined in Table 3 of the Final Report, and as shown 
above, are constructed by modifying the setup of Case 1MG and/or Case 1EG. 
  
• 
Case1-Earth Gravity-NS: (where NS stands for “no settling”) is identical to Case1-Earth Gravity 
except the settling mechanism has been neglected. 
• 
Case1-Earth Gravity-R: (where R stands for “rotated”) is identical to Case1-Earth Gravity except 
the heated vapor source has been rotated 90o. 
• 
Case1-Microgravity-NT: (where NT stands for “no thermophoresis”) is identical to Case1-
Microgrativy except the thermophoresis transport mechanism has been neglected.  
• 
Cases1Microgravity-R: (where R stands for “rotated”) is identical to Case1-Microgrativy except 
the heated vapor source has been rotated 90o.  
 
In all cases, data is collected and presented at a time of t = 7 seconds.
